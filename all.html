🖤 Gowtham Ramamurthy 🖤

Kamesh Ramamurthy (born 30 July 1996), professionally known as Gowtham Ramamurthy, entered this world on 30 July 1996 nearly 12:00 PM in Dhanvanthri Hospital, Trichy. He was born to Ramamurthy and Rajalakshmi. Family members say his first achievement was arriving with a big head 😂😂.

Gowtham was not an ordinary child—he was a certified troublemaker. His parents never had a peaceful day. His biggest childhood achievement? He reportedly tried to "eliminate" his brother twice! 😅 Thankfully, both attempts failed, and today they can laugh about those unforgettable childhood adventures. From a very young age, he fell in love with buses, bikes, and cars.

His hobbies include singing 🎤, driving 🚗, exploring automobiles 🚘, and listening to music 🎵.

He was selected by TCS, but fate had different plans because the offer letter never arrived 🥺. He joined Sutherland, then absconded from the company for some reason 🥺. After that, he joined Automas8 Company, where he has been working for more than 8 years 😊😊😊.

On 30 November 2025, Gowtham made the smartest decision of his life by marrying Aarthy Soundararajan ❤️. After marriage, life became much more interesting. He got a beautiful, caring, loving, and patient wife 😎 who somehow manages to tolerate all his drama, random singing, automobile talks, and occasional angry mode. He never expected to receive so much love and care from his wife, and honestly, he hit the jackpot 😜.

Today, they continue their journey together, collecting beautiful memories, endless laughter, a few funny arguments, and many adventures— with many more to come 🫶♾️.
